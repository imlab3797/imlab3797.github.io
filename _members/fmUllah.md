**Fath U Min Ullah** received his **Ph.D. in Software Convergence** from **Sejong University**, Seoul, South Korea, in **2023**. He is currently a **University Lecturer (Assistant Professor) in Artificial Intelligence** at the **School of Engineering and Computing, University of Lancashire**, Preston, United Kingdom.

Before joining the University of Lancashire, he worked as a **KTP Associate – AI Software Engineer (Vision Engineer)** at **Logically**, in collaboration with the **University of Sheffield**, United Kingdom. His research interests include **Computer Vision**, **Artificial Intelligence**, **Vision–Language Models**, **Large Language Models**, **Person Re-Identification**, **Human Activity Recognition**, **Industrial Anomaly Detection**, **Video Summarization**, **Time Series Analysis**, and **Multimedia Data Analysis**.
